# Kindle Keywords for Gravewater

## Recommended 7 Keywords:

1. **urban fantasy**
2. **dark fantasy**
3. **thief**
4. **heist**
5. **awakened powers**
6. **gritty fantasy**
7. **grimdark**

---

## Alternative Options (if you want to swap any):

- **magic system** (instead of awakened powers)
- **survival fantasy** (instead of grimdark)
- **city fantasy** (instead of urban fantasy)
- **corruption** (thematic keyword)
- **low fantasy** (if you want to emphasize the darker, less magical aspects)

---

## Notes:

- **urban fantasy** - Primary genre identifier
- **dark fantasy** - Tone and style
- **thief** - Main character/profession
- **heist** - Plot element (the Counting House theft)
- **awakened powers** - Magic system/unique element
- **gritty fantasy** - Tone descriptor
- **grimdark** - Subgenre that matches the dark, survival-focused tone

These keywords cover:
- Genre (urban fantasy, dark fantasy, grimdark)
- Character type (thief)
- Plot elements (heist)
- Unique elements (awakened powers)
- Tone (gritty)
