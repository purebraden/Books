# Gravewater - Book 1 Outline

## Book Information
- **Title:** Gravewater
- **Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Urban Fantasy
- **Word Count:** ~29,509 words
- **Estimated Pages:** ~108 pages
- **Structure:** Prologue + 35 Chapters + Epilogue

---

## Story Arc Overview

**Act I: The Crew (Prologue - Chapter 6)**
- Introduction to Kael and his crew
- Discovery of the dangerous paper
- Loss of Mirek
- Loss of Tessa
- Kael's power awakening

**Act II: The Power (Chapters 7-22)**
- Kael learns to use his new abilities
- The city begins to notice and react
- Escalating confrontations
- Kael becomes a problem the city can't ignore

**Act III: The Escape (Chapters 23-35)**
- The city's full response
- The thing below breaks containment
- Final confrontation and escape
- Kael leaves Gravewater behind

## Key Themes

1. **Survival vs. Living** - Kael learns there's more than just staying alive
2. **Power and Responsibility** - The knowing comes with costs and obligations
3. **The City as Character** - Gravewater is a living, breathing entity
4. **Loss and Growth** - Kael loses everything but gains power
5. **Escape and Freedom** - Leaving is both loss and gain

## Major Plot Points

1. **Inciting Incident:** Kael steals the schedule (Chapter 1)
2. **First Plot Point:** Mirek's death (Chapter 2)
3. **Second Plot Point:** Tessa's death and power awakening (Chapter 6)
4. **Midpoint:** Kael kills the closer (Chapter 18)
5. **Third Plot Point:** Containment fails (Chapter 33)
6. **Climax:** Kael escapes on the ship (Chapter 35)
7. **Resolution:** Kael at sea (Epilogue)
