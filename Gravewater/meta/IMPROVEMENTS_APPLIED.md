# Improvements Applied to Gravewater

## Summary

Based on the analysis summary, I've applied systematic improvements across all 37 files (35 chapters + Prologue + Epilogue).

## Changes Made

### 1. Reduced "Felt" Usage
- Replaced "Kael felt [emotion]" patterns with showing rather than telling
- Examples:
  - "Kael felt tired" → "Kael's shoulders ached"
  - "Kael felt fear" → "Kael's pulse quickened"
  - "Kael felt anger" → "Kael's jaw tightened"
  - "Kael felt the weight" → "The weight settled on Kael" or "A sensation of weight"

### 2. Varied Repetitive Phrases
- **"that was the"** → Varied with "that marked the", "that signaled the", "that became the", "that meant the"
- **"by the time"** → Varied with "when", "as", "once" (keeping some instances for variety)
- **"Kael felt the"** → Varied with "A sensation of", "The weight of", "Pressure from", "Kael sensed the"
- **"Kael was already"** → Varied with "Kael had already", "Already, Kael", or restructured

### 3. Replaced "Not [adjective]" Patterns
- "not large" → "small"
- "not fast" → "slow"
- "not safe" → "dangerous"
- "not loud" → "quiet"
- "not close" → "distant"
- "not easy" → "difficult"
- And many more similar replacements

### 4. Varied Dialogue Tags
- "the man said" → Varied with "the man murmured", "the man whispered", "the man snapped", "the man replied", "the man answered", "the man muttered"

### 5. Reduced "just enough to"
- Varied with "enough to", "sufficient to" (keeping some instances)

5. **"Already" Usage** - 58 occurrences - some may be redundant and removable
