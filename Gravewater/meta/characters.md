# Gravewater - Character Reference

## Main Characters

### Kael
- **Age:** Early twenties (exact age unknown)
- **Role:** Protagonist, thief, later becomes something more
- **Background:** 
  - Grew up as a nameless child in Gravewater's slums
  - Learned to count before he learned to read
  - Mastered survival through observation and stealth
- **Skills:**
  - Expert pickpocket and thief
  - Exceptional spatial awareness and planning
  - Ability to read people and situations
  - Master of moving unseen
- **Power:** "The Knowing" - awakened after Tessa's death
  - Can sense lies, debts, and obligations
  - Can move through space (teleportation-like ability)
  - Can speak words that have power to unravel people
  - Connected to unfinished business and debts
- **Personality:**
  - Pragmatic and survival-focused
  - Loyal to his crew (what's left of it)
  - Emotionally guarded but deeply feels loss
  - Adapts quickly to new situations
  - Prefers action to words
- **Relationships:**
  - Close bond with Tessa (deceased)
  - Partnership with Bram (status unknown)
  - Respected Mirek (deceased)
- **Arc:** From street thief to someone with power and responsibility, from survival to purpose

---

### Tessa
- **Age:** Similar to Kael
- **Role:** Kael's partner, crew member
- **Background:** Part of Kael's crew, skilled at watching and tracking
- **Skills:**
  - Expert at watching reflections and surfaces
  - Observes what things reflect, not what they show
  - Skilled with a knife
  - Good at reading situations
- **Personality:**
  - Quiet but observant
  - Brave and self-sacrificing
  - Close connection with Kael (possibly romantic, unspoken)
- **Fate:** Dies in Chapter 6, sacrificing herself to save Kael from the Tide-Slipper
- **Impact:** Her death awakens Kael's power and leaves him alone

---

### Mirek
- **Age:** Similar to Kael
- **Role:** Crew member, distraction specialist
- **Skills:**
  - Gift for drawing attention
  - Argues badly on purpose
  - Creates distractions for the crew
- **Personality:**
  - Loud and obvious
  - Grinning and energetic
  - Good at his role in the crew
- **Fate:** Found dead in Chapter 2, broken neck, officially called an accident
- **Impact:** First major loss, shows the crew is in real danger

---

### Bram
- **Age:** Similar to Kael
- **Role:** Crew member, Kael's closest remaining connection
- **Skills:**
  - Knows when questions cost more than answers
  - Quiet and observant
  - Reliable partner
- **Personality:**
  - Quiet and thoughtful
  - Understands when to ask and when to stay silent
  - Trustworthy
- **Status:** Unknown after the crew breaks apart
- **Potential:** Could return in future books

---

## Antagonists

### The Villain (Unnamed)
- **Role:** Primary antagonist, controls the city's systems
- **Characteristics:**
  - Operates through intermediaries
  - Controls the thing below (Tide-Slipper)
  - Has significant resources and influence
  - Wants to move problems rather than solve them
- **Methods:**
  - Uses the city's systems to control people
  - Sets up situations to blame Kael
  - Moves dangerous things to sea
- **Status:** Still active, organization unknown
- **Connection:** Connected to the thing below and the city's power structure

---

### The City (Gravewater)
- **Role:** Antagonist, living entity
- **Characteristics:**
  - Built to rot slowly
  - Routes around problems
  - Adjusts to contain threats
  - Has its own logic and systems
- **Methods:**
  - Closes routes
  - Routes around problems
  - Sets up situations to blame people
  - Moves on from problems
- **Philosophy:** Stay where you're put, or sink

---

## The Thing Below (Tide-Slipper)

### Characteristics
- **Type:** Supernatural entity/creature
- **Nature:** Imbalance given motion, state of wrongness
- **Abilities:**
  - Moves through water and reflections
  - Can pull people into water
  - Distorts space and reality around it
  - Learns and adapts
- **Behavior:**
  - Hunts and tests
  - Moves through Lowwake
  - Requires weight/balance to contain
- **Fate:** Destroyed by Kael in Chapter 6, but similar things may exist
- **Connection:** Connected to the city's foundations, possibly part of a larger system

## Character Arcs

### Kael's Arc
1. **Beginning:** Street thief, part of a crew, focused on survival
2. **Loss:** Loses Mirek, then Tessa
3. **Awakening:** Gains power but loses everything
4. **Isolation:** Becomes a problem the city can't ignore
5. **Escape:** Leaves Gravewater, gains freedom but loses home

### Tessa's Arc
1. **Beginning:** Part of the crew, Kael's partner
2. **Investigation:** Helps investigate the thing below
3. **Sacrifice:** Dies saving Kael
4. **Impact:** Her death awakens Kael's power

### The City's Arc
1. **Beginning:** Ignores Kael
2. **Recognition:** Begins to notice and test Kael
3. **Containment:** Tries to contain and control Kael
4. **Isolation:** Routes around Kael, tries to make him leave
5. **Failure:** Cannot catch or control Kael
6. **Resolution:** Kael escapes, city moves on
