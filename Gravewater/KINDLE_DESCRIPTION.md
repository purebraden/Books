# Kindle Book Description for Gravewater

## Option 1: Atmospheric & Gritty (Recommended)

In the waterlogged city of Gravewater, every debt comes due. Kael learned this lesson early—stealing bread at eight, running jobs with his crew by sixteen, watching the city's ledgers balance themselves in blood and coin.

But when a simple heist goes wrong, Kael's crew begins to die. First Mirek, found broken in the canals. Then Tessa, pulled into the dark water by something that shouldn't exist. As Kael's world fractures, something awakens inside him—a power that shows him the hidden debts binding Gravewater together, the costs quietly paid and those left unpaid.

The city notices.

Now hunted by forces that see him as both threat and asset, Kael must navigate a web of corruption, awakened powers, and the city's own malevolent will. His only chance at freedom lies in one final theft—a heist so audacious it will force Gravewater to bleed openly. But escape from the city is only the beginning. The sea beyond holds dangers of its own, and the price of freedom may be higher than Kael can afford to pay.

A dark urban fantasy where survival is measured in debts and the city itself keeps accounts.

## Option 2: More Action-Focused

Kael is a thief in Gravewater, a city built on water and corruption. When his crew starts dying—first Mirek, then Tessa—Kael awakens to a power that shows him the hidden debts and obligations binding the city together. But awakening makes him a target.

Hunted by the Watch, the awakened, and the city's own malevolent will, Kael must pull off one final heist to buy his freedom. The target: the Counting House vault, where the city's corruption is stored in gold and sealed documents. Success means escape. Failure means death.

But even if he escapes Gravewater, the sea beyond holds new dangers—and awakened pirates who have their own plans for a city-shadow with his talents.

A gritty urban fantasy of survival, power, and the cost of freedom.

## Option 3: Shorter & Punchy

Gravewater taught Kael one lesson: every debt comes due.

A thief since childhood, Kael runs jobs with his crew until a heist goes wrong and his friends start dying. When his power awakens—showing him the hidden debts binding the city—Gravewater marks him as both threat and asset.

Hunted by forces that see him as a weapon, Kael plans one final theft: the Counting House vault, where the city's corruption is stored. It's a heist that will either buy his freedom or end his life.

But escape from Gravewater is only the beginning. The sea beyond holds awakened pirates, and freedom has a price Kael may not be able to pay.

A dark urban fantasy where survival is measured in debts and the city keeps accounts.

## Option 4: Character-Driven

Kael learned hunger before he learned numbers. In Gravewater, a city built on water and corruption, survival means learning which debts to pay and which to avoid.

When a job goes wrong and his crew begins to die, Kael awakens to a power that shows him the hidden obligations binding the city—who owes what, and what happens when debts go unpaid. But awakening makes him visible to forces that want to use him, control him, or eliminate him.

With the city closing in, Kael must pull off one final heist: steal from the Counting House vault, where Gravewater stores its corruption. Success means escape. Failure means death.

But the sea beyond Gravewater holds its own dangers, and freedom may cost more than Kael is willing to pay.

A dark urban fantasy about survival, power, and the price of escape.
